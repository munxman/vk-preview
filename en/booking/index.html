Doctor's appointment without a prior booking

Fast, quality care for urgent and chronic health issues, also available outside working hours and on Saturdays.

Booking

Opening hours: Mon–Fri 9:00–18:00; Sat 12:00–16:00.

Please note: If you miss your gynaecologist, paediatrician or mental health specialist appointment or cancel with less than 24 hours' notice, the clinic reserves the right to charge 50% of the visit fee.

We recommend booking in advance online or by phone.

How to book

1

Select a service

Choose the required service from the dropdown in the booking widget below.

2

Choose a time

Pick a convenient available slot. Same-day GP and nurse appointments are available.

3

Confirm your booking

Enter your contact details and confirm. No registration required.

Note: The booking system displays service names in Estonian. We offer same-day appointments with a general practitioner and nurse. Specialists are available according to schedule and prior booking is required.

Our services

Fast, quality medical care in Tallinn. Same-day appointments available, including Saturdays.

🩺

Doctor's appointment

Valvekliinik's experienced general and specialised doctors and nurses welcome patients for unexpected health issues, diagnosing and treatment of chronic illnesses, and health issue prevention questions. Our family practitioners, general and specialized doctors, and nurses goal is to always offer the best help with a friendly and personal approach.

Learn more
🌸

Gynaecology

The gynecologists of Valvekliinik deal with all gynecological problems. Our goal is to provide professional help as soon as a woman needs it. Patients turn to us with questions that need a quick solution such as problems with vaginal flow, stomach pain, menstrual cycle issues, pregnancy confirmation, issues during pregnancy, and questions about contraception. We also advise on menopausal transition issues.

Learn more
🤱

Pregnancy Centre

Our Gynaecology and Pregnancy Center offers pregnant women the option of all necessary ultrasound and other pregnancy-related tests. Valvekliinik offers pregnancy monitoring and personal care during the whole pregnancy. We welcome women from the early stages of pregnancy so we can assist with performing all necessary tests and ensure the health of mother and baby.

Learn more
🔬

Ultrasound examinations

We offer different ultrasound examinations to assess the size, construction and structure of organs, the conditions of joints, and the speed and direction of blood flow. Abdominal ultrasound, thyroid ultrasound, complex ultrasound, vein and soft tissue ultrasound. No referral needed; adults and children welcome.

Learn more
🧪

Lab tests

Most of the necessary tests and examinations can be performed on-site at Valvekliinik: blood, urine analyses and rapid tests, including STI testing. You can give tests quickly without a prior appointment Mon–Fri 9:00–18:00 and Sat 12:00–16:00.

Learn more
🛠️

Procedures

Our experienced doctors and nurses offer a wide variety of on-site procedures such as ECG, ear wax removal, ultrasound examinations and vaccination. We also complete minor surgical procedures on-site such as removal of ingrown nails, birth marks, or skin formations, abscess incision and drainage, and atheroma removal.

Learn more
📋

Health certificates

Valvekliinik offers same-day appointments for various health certificates — including those required for driving a motor vehicle, employment, insurance, and other purposes. If there are no health concerns that would prevent issuance, we can issue the certificate on the same day. Please bring a valid identity document.

Prescription renewal

Valvekliinik renews the prescription on the same day. To renew your prescription, please fill in the form on our website, email us at info@valvekliinik.ee, or call +372 5911 0909. A standard prescription costs €15 and is processed during regular hours. Express prescriptions are issued within two hours of payment confirmation.

Learn more
⭐

Concierge – Personal doctor

Valvekliinik offers a concierge service – a personal doctor service which is an increasingly popular high-quality healthcare service. We believe that to have good health, one needs to take constant care of themselves. Visiting the same doctor helps achieve good communication and gives the opportunity for the doctor and patient to build a long-term relationship of trust.

Learn more
🧠

Mental health

Valvekliinik provides professional support for mental health concerns. A psychologist-counselor can help with various emotional and psychological issues that affect well-being, relationships with loved ones, and coping with everyday life. We recommend contacting our specialist for difficulties with concentration and motivation, anxiety, fear, loneliness, low self-esteem, and more.

Driving licence medical certificate

Health check and certificate issued in one short visit. The certificate is sent to the Transport Administration automatically.

🚗

Category I

Categories A, B, AM, T and their sub-categories. Standard car, motorcycle, moped, tractor.

from €65
🚛

Category II

Categories C, D, E and their sub-categories. Lorry, bus, trailer – professional licence categories.

from €90

Three steps to your certificate

1

Before your visit: complete the health declaration

Go to terviseportaal.ee and complete the health declaration using Smart-ID or your ID card. This is the mandatory first step for the e-health certificate application and must be done before your appointment. It can also be completed at the clinic.

2

Come in for your health check

The health check takes approximately 20–30 minutes. We measure blood pressure, vision, weight, height, neck circumference and blood glucose. Bring a valid identity document and your glasses (if you wear them). Come on an empty stomach – no food for at least 2 hours before the appointment (water is fine).

3

Certificate sent automatically to the Transport Administration

The certificate is issued digitally and sent automatically to the vehicle register. No paperwork needed – everything is handled electronically. The certificate is valid immediately upon issue.

What is examined?

  • Blood pressure
  • Vision
  • Body mass index (weight and height)
  • Neck circumference
  • Blood glucose
  • Transport Administration requirements

What to bring?

  • A valid identity document (ID card or passport)
  • Glasses or contact lenses if you wear them – the vision check is performed with them on
  • Preferably a completed health declaration via terviseportaal.ee (can also be completed at the clinic)
  • Come on an empty stomach – no food for at least 2 hours before your appointment (water is fine)

Frequently asked questions

Can I get the driving licence medical certificate the same day?

Yes. If you have completed the health declaration in advance via terviseportaal.ee (can also be done at the clinic) and no issues are found during the health check, the certificate is issued the same day. It is sent to the Transport Administration digitally.

Is a referral required?

No, no referral is needed. Simply contact us directly – book online or call +372 5911 0909.

What is the health declaration and where do I complete it?

The health declaration is the mandatory first step for applying for an e-health certificate. Complete it before your visit at terviseportaal.ee using Smart-ID or your ID card. A certificate cannot be issued without a completed declaration.

How long does the driving licence health check take?

The health check takes approximately 20–30 minutes. Including any waiting time, please allow around 30 minutes for the visit.

Do I need to come on an empty stomach?

Yes. Please avoid eating for at least 2 hours before your appointment, as we measure blood glucose. Still water is fine.

How often does the driving licence medical certificate need to be renewed?

Category I (A, B, AM, T) – every 10 years for those under 65. Category II (C, D, E) – every 5 years. Drivers aged 65 and over must renew the certificate every 5 years for all categories.

Is the clinic open on Saturdays?

Yes. We are open on Saturdays 12:00–16:00 and on public holidays 12:00–16:00. Monday to Friday we are open 9:00–18:00.

Where is the certificate sent?

The certificate is issued digitally and sent automatically to the vehicle register. You do not need to submit anything separately – everything is handled electronically immediately after the certificate is issued.

What happens if a health issue is found during the check?

If the health check reveals that the certificate cannot be issued, we will inform you accordingly. The consultation fee is non-refundable, as the health check has been carried out. In such cases, we can advise you on next steps.

Can I pay by card?

Yes, payment is accepted on-site by card or in cash.
